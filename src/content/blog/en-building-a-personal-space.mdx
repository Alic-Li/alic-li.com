A personal website can be a useful kind of notebook: a place for finished work, questions in progress, and the things learned in between.

This first iteration starts with a simple idea: keep the content clear, and make the surrounding interface feel quiet.

## Static where possible

Most of this site is HTML generated ahead of time. Astro keeps interactive React components in small islands: appearance controls, a visual experiment, and the chat playground.

```ts
// Content is static. Only the API needs a Worker.
export const prerender = false;
```

That boundary makes the site easy to serve and keeps the reading experience independent of JavaScript.

## Two languages, one space

English lives at `/`, and Simplified Chinese at `/zh/`. The language control keeps the current page. Your browser’s language never causes an automatic redirect.

## A little glass, a lot of clarity

Translucent surfaces work best when they have a purpose. Here, they give the navigation a subtle separation from the page. Stronger visual effects are decorative enhancements, with a simpler fallback for browsers and accessibility preferences.

## What comes next

The foundation is here. The collection of projects, notes, and experiments can grow at its own pace.
