Meet my RWKV mascot, RWKV v4 · Raven 🐦‍⬛.

She always says, “Just wait until I grow up. Then you’ll see!”

Yet after all these years, she is still that same little raven.

An outsider, stubborn, and sometimes a little fierce. When others laugh at her, she clutches her thick stack of notes and retreats to a corner of the archive shelves, quietly learning on her own.

She has few resources, and few people believe in her.

**She never grew bigger, but she never stopped growing.**

_Still here. Still learning._
